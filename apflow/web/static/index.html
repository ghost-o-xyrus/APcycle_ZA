    Part 1

    What is here and how to test it

    Two systems on one site

    Invoice desk (this app)
    Takes an invoice PDF and decides: approve, hold for a person (naming who and what to ask) or reject (with a message the vendor can act on). You watch each step as it runs. The Dashboard totals what has been processed, and is where you choose which model reads scans.
    Kaveri ERP
    The buyer's system of record, standing in for NetSuite or SAP: the vendor master, purchase orders and goods receipts, each with its printed document. The desk keeps no copy; on every run it reads them through the ERP's API with an integration token, as an AP product reads a client's ERP.
    The reader
    PDFs with a text layer are read on the server, with no model. Scans go to the reader chosen on the Dashboard: a free cloud model (Gemini), a model on your computer, the demo stand-in (which knows only the built-in sample scans), or OCR alone. The model only reads; every decision comes from fixed rules.

    The buyer is an invented group, Kaveri Instruments, with companies in India (rupees), the US (dollars) and Germany (euros).

    Test it with the data provided

    1. Open Process an invoice and pick a sample from the list on the left. Samples are grouped: clean invoices that should be approved, edge cases, international invoices, and documents that are not invoices. Items marked scan are images and need the reader.
    2. Watch the run. Each of the eight steps shows what it found. The stamp on the page is the decision.
    3. Read the tabs under the run. All checks lists every check (17 for an Indian invoice) with its evidence; What was read shows each field and how sure the reader was; Three-way match sets each invoice line against its PO line and goods receipt, with links to those documents in the ERP.
    4. Act on a hold. Approve, approve part, confirm the suggested PO, or reject, with a reason. The action is recorded in the run's History.
    5. Fill the dashboard and grade it. On the Dashboard, press Run all samples (30: 25 made up, 5 real). Under All runs, press Check against expected outcomes: each sample shows the decision it was designed to get and whether it got it, with a count at the top. Two exceptions are allowed for: a sample run again after it was approved is a duplicate and should be rejected, and a scan read by OCR alone may be held for a person. Clear all runs starts again.
    6. Try the cases below with Run this case.

    To try the upload path with the same files, download a sample and drop it on Upload an invoice PDF: . The POs and goods receipts behind them are in the ERP; each record there opens its PDF.

    The process

    Edge cases built in

    Each one needs information beyond the invoice itself, which is where checking by hand usually fails.

    Rules and thresholds

    Read live from the system's settings.

    Assumptions

    • The buyer is a group of three companies: Kaveri Instruments Pvt Ltd (India, rupees), Kaveri Instruments Inc. (US, dollars) and Kaveri Instruments GmbH (Germany, euros). The billed company comes from the PO, the tax number or the name on the invoice.
    • Tax follows where the supplier and the billed company are: GST in India (CGST + SGST within a state, IGST across states); no GST on an import into India (IGST is paid at customs); US sales tax; domestic VAT; reverse charge between EU businesses.
    • One approval limit for the whole group, in rupees, converting at fixed demo rates (1 USD = ₹84, 1 EUR = ₹91). A live system would take Treasury's rate for the day.
    • Most invoices do not print a PO number (about 1 in 5 real scans did), so the PO is found by matching the vendor's open POs.
    • Government (GeM-style) POs quote prices including tax; invoices show tax separately. Prices are compared before tax.
    • Goods are matched three ways (PO, goods receipt, invoice); services two ways (PO, invoice).
    • POs, goods receipts and the vendor master live in the company's ERP, not in the desk. Kaveri ERP stands in for NetSuite or SAP: its API is shaped like NetSuite's and needs an integration token, and the desk reads it on every run. Testers can add their own vendor, PO and goods receipt there (open Kaveri ERP).
    • One file is one invoice. Bundled invoices, or pages that were not read, go to a person.
    • The ERP's vendor master, POs and goods receipts are a realistic invented company, each with a printed document; one real public GeM order is kept there for reference. The sample invoices are synthetic.

    What it does not do

    • Pay vendors, post approved bills back to the ERP, or code invoices to ledger accounts.
    • Read languages other than English. Tax rules cover India, the US and the EU; GSTINs, EINs and German and French VAT IDs are checked for format and check digits, but not looked up in the EU's VIES register.
    • Sign people in: whoever acts on a hold types their name.
    • Keep history across restarts on the free host: runs and records added in the ERP reset when the service restarts after being idle.
    • The live demo reads scans with a free cloud tier, which may keep and review requests. That is fine for synthetic invoices. For real invoices, use a model on your own computer or server (part 3), or a paid tier.

    Part 2

    Your own PO, goods receipt and invoice

    An invoice is approved only when the ERP holds a purchase order that agrees with it and, for goods, a goods receipt showing the goods arrived and were accepted. Upload your own invoice without them and it is held: no matching PO. So put the PO and receipt into the ERP first, in this order.

    Files: PDF only, up to 15 MB each, for invoices, POs and receipts alike. Text PDFs are read fully up to 5 pages; scanned PDFs, the first 2 pages by a model or the first 3 by OCR. One file is one document. English only.
    1. Check the vendor is in the vendor master. Open ERP > Vendors. If the supplier is listed, note its name and go on. If not, add it here with Add a vendor, or while adding the PO (next step). A vendor needs:
      NameAs printed on their invoices.
      CountryIndia, United States, Germany, France or United Kingdom.
      Tax numberIndia: GSTIN, required. 15 characters, the last a check character (29ABCDE1234F1ZW is a valid made-up one). The first two digits give the state, which decides CGST and SGST against IGST.
      US: EIN, 12-3456789. Germany: VAT ID DE and 9 digits, check digit verified. France: FR, a 2-character key and 9 digits, key verified. Others: format only. Outside India the tax number may be left blank, but an EU vendor without a VAT ID has its invoices held.
      Bank accountAccount number (India, US) or IBAN (Europe), exactly as on their invoices. An invoice naming a different account is held for fraud review.
      Bank codeIFSC (India), routing number (US) or BIC (Europe).
      Payment termsDays, 30 if left alone.
      A GSTIN already used by another vendor is refused.
    2. Add the purchase order. Open ERP > Purchase orders > Add a PO and drop the PO's PDF on the upload box. The ERP reads it into the form (a text PDF on the server; a scan with the chosen reader). Check every field before saving: the desk holds the invoice to exactly what is saved. You can also skip the upload and type the PO in.
      PO numberAs on the PO; must be unique. Leave blank to get the next number (PO-2026-9001 and on). The invoice need not quote it: the desk finds the PO by vendor and lines when it is missing.
      PO dateThe invoice may not be dated before it.
      Ordered byWhich group company is buying: India, US or Germany. It decides the tax rules and the buyer details the invoice must show.
      CurrencyINR, USD, EUR or GBP. The invoice must be in the same currency.
      KindGoods need a goods receipt (three-way match). Services are matched to the PO alone (two-way).
      Prices on the PO areExcluding tax, or including tax (as government GeM orders print them). The desk compares prices before tax either way.
      VendorPick from the list, or choose New vendor and fill in the vendor fields above.
      LinesFor each line: description, HSN code (optional), quantity (above 0), unit, rate (not negative) and tax rate (0 to 40%). Use the descriptions the invoice will use: invoice lines are paired with PO lines by description, and by HSN code when both have one.
      Saved POs are open for receipts and invoices.
    3. Record the goods receipt (goods only). Open ERP > Goods receipts > Record a goods receipt, choose the PO, and drop the receipt PDF or type the quantities per line:
      ReceivedWhat arrived.
      AcceptedWhat passed inspection; never more than received. Only accepted goods can be paid for.
      LimitsOpen POs only. All receipts on a line together may reach 110% of the quantity ordered, no more. Several receipts per PO are fine (part deliveries).
      Receipt number, date, byBlank number: the next one (GRN-9001 and on).
    4. Upload the invoice on Process an invoice: drop the PDF on Upload an invoice PDF. Up to 15 MB. The run reads the ERP as it is at that moment.
    5. If it was held because the ERP lacked something, add it in the ERP and press Check again on the run. The desk reads the ERP afresh and decides again.

    Testing a real invoice of your own

    A real invoice is addressed to a real buyer, not to Kaveri. AP pays invoices for the company they are addressed to, so add that buyer first, then the same steps as above:

    1. Add the buyer under ERP > Companies > Add a test company: the name under Bill to or Sold to, exactly as printed, its country, and its tax number if the invoice shows one.
    2. Add the vendor, as in step 1 above, with the name printed at the top of the invoice. Outside India, the tax number and bank details may be left blank if the invoice has none.
    3. Add the PO with Ordered by set to your test company. To see an approval, copy the invoice's lines as they are printed. To test a check, change one thing on purpose: a lower rate, a smaller quantity.
    4. For goods, record a goods receipt; accept fewer than billed to see the quantity check hold it.
    5. Upload the invoice with Gemini or your own model selected as the reader; most real invoices need one.

    If the invoice prints no PO number, the desk finds the PO by vendor and lines and asks AP to confirm it: press Confirm on the run, and the checks run again.

    What counts as agreeing

    VendorThe invoice's GSTIN or tax number (or failing that, its name) matches an approved vendor, and its bank account matches the one on file.
    BuyerThe invoice is addressed to the company on the PO, with that company's tax number when the rules need one.
    PriceEach unit price, before tax, within ₹1 per unit or 0.5% of the PO rate, whichever is larger (1 unit of the invoice's currency for dollars or euros).
    QuantityNot more than accepted on goods receipts, minus anything already billed on earlier invoices.
    ValueAll invoices against the PO together stay within its value; an excess is held for Procurement.
    Tax and totalsTax at the PO's rate and of the right kind (CGST+SGST, IGST, sales tax, VAT or reverse charge); lines, subtotal, tax and total add up within ₹1.
    ApprovalAbove ₹5,00,000 before tax (other currencies converted at the demo rates) a manager must sign off.

    Tidying up

    • Records you add are tagged tester. Remove tester records on the ERP's home page deletes them; the demo company's records stay.
    • The free host restarts after about 15 minutes idle, which also clears tester records and runs. Expect a slow first page after a pause.

    For integrators: the ERP's API

    REST, shaped like NetSuite's: /erp/api/record/v1/vendor, /purchaseOrder, /itemReceipt, each with /<id>, and /purchaseOrder/<id>/!transform/itemReceipt. Every call needs Authorization: Bearer <token>; without it the answer is 401. The token is on the ERP's Integration page.

    Part 3

    A private model on your computer

    Scans need a vision model to be read well. Instead of the cloud model, you can run an open model on your own computer. This site is hosted elsewhere and cannot reach your computer, but this page, open in your browser, can: the desk hands each scan to your browser, your browser passes it to the model on your computer and returns the answer. No AI company sees the page.

    Be aware: the PDF itself is still uploaded to this site to be checked. For documents that must not leave your company at all, run the whole desk on your own machine (below).

    What you need

    ComputerWindows 10 or 11, macOS 12 or later, or Linux. 8 GB of memory at least (the model uses about 6 GB; 16 GB is comfortable). About 5 GB of free disk. A graphics card helps but is not needed.
    BrowserChrome or Edge, recent. They ask your permission before a site can reach your computer. Firefox also works. Safari may block it; if nothing is found in Safari, use Chrome.
    Python3.9 or later, for the launcher script. Check with python --version (on Windows also py --version). If missing: python.org/downloads (on Windows, tick Add python.exe to PATH).
    TimeAbout 15 minutes the first time, most of it the download (about 3.4 GB). Later starts take a minute.
    SpeedWith a recent graphics card, seconds per invoice. On a laptop processor, one to several minutes per scan. Text PDFs never use the model.

    Choose a model

    NuExtract3 (Numind), 4BBuilt for pulling fields out of documents into a fixed form. The default. Runs best under llama.cpp. Download about 3.4 GB (2.7 GB model and 0.7 GB image encoder). Apache-2.0.
    Qwen3-VL 4B (Alibaba)A general model that reads images, asked for the desk's JSON. Runs best under Ollama. Download about 3.3 GB. Apache-2.0.

    Steps: NuExtract3 with llama.cpp

    1. Install llama.cpp. Open a terminal (Windows: Terminal or PowerShell; macOS: Terminal) and run 
      winget install llama.cpp
      on Windows, or 
      brew install llama.cpp
      on macOS or Linux (with Homebrew). Close the terminal and open a new one, then check: 
      llama-server --version
    2. Download the launcher, a small Python script that picks a free port, starts the model and lets this site talk to it: Download serve_reader.py
    3. Run it from the folder it was saved to: 
      cd Downloads
python serve_reader.py
      (on Windows py serve_reader.py if python is not found). The first run downloads the model; the window shows progress messages every 20 seconds.
    4. Wait for [reader] ready: NuExtract3 at http://127.0.0.1:8100 (the port may differ) and checked: a page at https://apcycle-za.onrender.com may call this model. Leave this window open: closing it stops the model.
    5. Connect it. On the Dashboard, under Use a model on your computer, press Find models on my computer. If the browser asks to let this site reach devices on your local network or apps on this device, choose Allow. The desk connects to the model and selects it as the reader.
    6. Test it. Press Test the reader on the Dashboard: two sample scans go to your model and its reading is compared with their answer keys. Then run any scanned sample.

    Steps: Qwen3-VL 4B with Ollama

    1. Install Ollama from ollama.com/download.
    2. Download the launcher (serve_reader.py) and run, from the folder it was saved to: 
      python serve_reader.py --model qwen3-vl
      It starts a separate Ollama on a free port that allows this site, and downloads qwen3-vl:4b (about 3.3 GB) the first time. An Ollama you already run is left alone; both share downloaded models.
    3. Wait for ready: qwen3-vl:4b, then connect and test on the Dashboard as in steps 5 and 6 above.
    Without the launcher: your usual Ollama

    Ollama refuses requests from web pages unless they are allowed. Allow this site, restart Ollama, and download the model:

    Windows
    setx OLLAMA_ORIGINS "https://apcycle-za.onrender.com"
    then quit Ollama from the tray icon (right-click, Quit) and start it again from the Start menu.
    macOS
    launchctl setenv OLLAMA_ORIGINS "https://apcycle-za.onrender.com"
    then quit and reopen the Ollama app.
    Linux
    OLLAMA_ORIGINS="https://apcycle-za.onrender.com" ollama serve
    (for the system service, add Environment="OLLAMA_ORIGINS=https://apcycle-za.onrender.com" with sudo systemctl edit ollama, then restart it).

    Then ollama pull qwen3-vl:4b. Find models on my computer looks on Ollama's usual port, 11434.

    Without the launcher: llama.cpp by hand 
    llama-server -hf numind/NuExtract3-GGUF:Q4_K_M --jinja --port 8100

    llama.cpp's server accepts requests from web pages by default. For Qwen3-VL use -hf Qwen/Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct-GGUF:Q4_K_M.

    Your own model

    Any model that reads images, served with the OpenAI-style API (/v1/models and /v1/chat/completions) or by Ollama, can be used:

    • Allow this site (CORS) to call it: LM Studio: turn on Enable CORS in the server settings. vLLM: --allowed-origins '["https://apcycle-za.onrender.com"]'. Ollama: OLLAMA_ORIGINS, as above. llama.cpp: allowed already. Or start any server through the launcher with --cmd "your-server --host {host} --port {port}", which picks the port and checks the result.
    • Name: the desk recognises image models by name: NuExtract, Qwen-VL, LLaVA, Gemma 3, Pixtral, MiniCPM-V, InternVL, Granite Vision, Moondream, or names containing vl, vision or ocr. NuExtract models are given NuExtract's extraction template; others are given the desk's instructions and asked for JSON.
    • Where it is: the search looks at ports 8100 to 8120 and the usual ones (11434 Ollama, 1234 LM Studio, 8080 and 8081 llama.cpp, 8000 and 8001 vLLM, 30000, 5000, 5001) on this computer. Anywhere else, type its address, for example http://127.0.0.1:9000 or another machine on your network, and press Connect.
    • Then press Test the reader to see how well it reads before trusting it.

    If it does not work

    Nothing foundIs the launcher's window still open and showing ready? A first download takes a while. Is the port within 8100 to 8120? If not, type the address shown.
    The browser blocked itClick the icon at the left of the address bar, open the site's settings, and set the local network (or apps on this device) permission to Allow. Reload and try again.
    Ollama found nothing, or is refusedYour Ollama was started without allowing this site: see Without the launcher above, or use the launcher. Check it has an image model: ollama list.
    Found, but "cannot read images"The server is serving a text-only model. Load NuExtract3, Qwen3-VL or another vision model.
    Slow, or the run says it used OCRA run waits up to 10 minutes for your model, then reads with OCR instead and says so. On a laptop, try one scan at a time. Keep the desk open in a tab: if every desk tab is closed, scans go to OCR until you connect again.
    "No longer connected"The desk restarted (the free host sleeps when idle) or another tab connected a model. Press Find models on my computer again.
    Port already in useThe launcher skips busy ports by itself. To choose the range: --ports 8100-8120. python serve_reader.py --list shows the models it started.

    Running the whole desk yourself

    For full privacy, run the desk on your own computer or server, with Python 3.13, Tesseract OCR and Poppler installed: pip install -r requirements-app.txt, then python -m apflow.web.app, and open http://localhost:8000. Models are then connected the same way (the launcher already allows http://localhost:8000). On the same machine as the models you can also let the server look for them itself by starting it with APFLOW_SERVER_READERS=1. The repository's README has the details.
